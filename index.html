Designed to disappear

Everything you need. Nothing that asks for attention.

01 · Independent faders

Every sound on its own fader.

Bring the rain forward, let the surf sit low, add a whisper of wind. Each layer has its own volume, and changes glide rather than jump.

02 · Gapless

Loops you'll never notice.

Each recording's end is blended into its beginning, so it plays on without a gap, click or seam.

03 · Remembered

Your mix, kept.

Lull saves your balance in this browser and restores it on your next visit. Nothing is sent anywhere.

04 · Considerate

Silent until you're ready.

No surprise autoplay. Sound begins only when you press play, then fades in softly.

05 · For everyone

Any screen. Any hand.

Works on a bedside phone or a desk monitor, from the keyboard, with clear screen-reader labels.

How it works

Three steps to a quieter mind.

  1. 01

    Choose your sounds

    Tap any sound to bring it in. Start with rain. It's a good place to begin.

  2. 02

    Find the balance

    Slide each layer up or down until nothing stands out and everything belongs.

  3. 03

    Let it run

    Work, read or drift off. The soundscape plays on seamlessly for as long as you need.

The library

Nine sounds, endless rooms.

Each sound is chosen to blend with the others. Nothing sharp, nothing that pulls your attention.
